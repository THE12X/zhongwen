About us

Zhongwen (中文) exists for one reason: everyone should be able to learn Chinese without paying for it. Every page, word list and quiz here is free, with no accounts and no ads.

Our vocabulary follows the HSK levels 1 to 4, the standard path most learners take, and each word comes with pinyin and an English meaning. Use the Vocabulary page to study and the Practice page to check what stuck.

Found a mistake or want a word added? Tell us and we will fix it.
